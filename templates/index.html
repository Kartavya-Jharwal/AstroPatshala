About Chaldean Numerology

Chaldean numerology is the oldest known numerological system, originating over 4,000 years ago in ancient Babylon. Unlike other systems, it assigns numbers based on vibrational frequencies and considers the number 9 sacred, excluding it from the letter-to-number chart except in final totals.
